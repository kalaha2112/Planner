Two directions for the planner

Same app, same components — only the skin differs. A · Sunset Express is a retro travel poster: flat saturated colour on warm cream, hard offset shadows, chunky type. B · Night Market is neon signage after dark: acid lime, amber and papaya on a green-black, gradient accents, frosted glass. Neither palette uses blue, pink or purple. Each is shown in light and dark.
To drive the real app instead, open index.html?skin=pop or index.html?skin=neon (?skin=classic restores today's look).
